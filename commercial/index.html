Food Service Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a critical concern for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained kitchen to health inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the records many leases and inspectors ask for.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged correctly, and adjust mixing valves so water stored at higher temperatures leaves the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

Each hour without hot water costs a business money. In a restaurant, it can bring the dish room to a halt. For a salon, it cancels appointments. For a hotel, it means complaints at the front desk, and in a multifamily building it leads to tenant disputes. Elk Grove Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater repair for commercial properties throughout the area, with after-hours scheduling for planned work so you don't lose a business day.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless unit stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Elk Grove Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Fewer outages, longer equipment life, lower energy bills: that's what a maintenance program is built to deliver. A program typically costs a fraction of a single emergency replacement. Contact Elk Grove Water Heater to set up a plan around your property's needs and budget.
